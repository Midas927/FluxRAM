## Page Flow

1. Hero: FluxRAM name, literal offer, domestic Portable download, current version.
2. Memory state: interactive explanation of active, protected, idle, and releasable groups.
3. Two actions: ordinary Boost versus Deep Release, with explicit boundaries.
4. Safety contract: what is excluded, what must be observed, what requires confirmation.
5. Editions: compact Free/Pro comparison and purchase entry.
6. Download hub: Portable, Lite, SHA256, GitHub fallback.
7. FAQ: SSD, FPS, low yield, unknown publisher.
8. Footer: source, releases, issues.

## Mobile Priority

1. Product name and domestic download.

3. Boost / Deep Release distinction.
4. Download choices.
5. Remaining proof and FAQ.
