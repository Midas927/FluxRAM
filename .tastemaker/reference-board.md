## Reusable DNA

- Bright technical paper ground with dark evidence strips.
- Strict editorial rules and numbered sections.
- Dense but readable process tables as proof, not fabricated benchmarks.
- Emerald action, blue informational state, orange caution.
- Dark, low-contrast memory blocks may fill the hero background, but must not dictate the foreground layout.

## Anti References

- Generic black and neon-green dashboard clone.
- Purple gradient SaaS hero.
- Full-screen product screenshot as a background.
- Repeated rounded feature-card wall.
- Restoring the entire old homepage when only its background was requested.
